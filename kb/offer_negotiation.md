# Offer Negotiation Policy

Initial compensation offers generated by the talent acquisition team are anchored to the median band of the target role grade and verified prior payslips. Candidates may submit a single formal counter-proposal within 5 business days of receiving the provisional offer letter. Any compensation adjustment exceeding 12 percent above the initial base salary offer requires written sign-off from the Vice President of Human Resources. Joining bonuses are capped at 150000 INR and are subject to a 12-month clawback agreement if the employee resigns voluntarily.
