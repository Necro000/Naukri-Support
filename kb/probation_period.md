# Probation Period Policy

Every newly hired full-time employee enters a mandatory 90-day probation evaluation period starting from their official date of joining. Managers conduct structured check-in reviews at day 30, day 60, and day 85 to evaluate role competency, deliverables, and team alignment. If performance goals are not met by day 85, the probation window may be extended once for an additional 30 calendar days with documented milestones. During probation, either party may terminate employment with a shortened 15-day written notice or equivalent base salary in lieu.
